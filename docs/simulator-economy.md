# Simulator economy ledger

Include engine/simulator/economy.hpp and link WebEngine::Simulator. Ledger is an optional standard-C++20, single-owner-thread helper. Prices, currencies, opening funds, wage accrual, refund eligibility and effect coordination belong to the application. It adds no renderer, clock, platform, IO or accounting-service dependency.

Ledger(opening=0, historyLimit=128, streamLimit=16, moneyLimit=1000000000) supports positive credit and negative debit postings in integer minor units. Cash stays in [0, moneyLimit]. Cumulative credits and debits each stay within moneyLimit. The configurable ceiling is at most INT64_MAX/4 so derived balances are checked without overflow. History is 1-4096 recent entries; stream count is 1-64. Stream keys are owned namespaced ASCII identifiers up to 64 characters. A posting needs a nonzero signed amount, nonnegative nondecreasing simulation time and a source sequence exactly one greater than its stream watermark, starting at one. Replayed sequences return AlreadyTerminal; gaps, invalid input, insufficient cash and overflow publish nothing.

post(stream, sequence, amount, time) is the convenience path. prepare(...) builds a move-only Prepared candidate containing all allocations and validation, while leaving live balances unchanged. commit(std::move(prepared)) is noexcept and publishes with no allocations or callbacks. It rejects foreign, consumed and stale candidates with StaleTransaction. Every posting and restore changes the revision. A retained shared lifetime token also rejects quotes after ledger destruction and address reuse. Ledger is noncopyable/nonmovable; discard a prepared candidate to cancel it. Borrowed stream/history views expire on publication.

Applications can prepare a cash posting, perform an existing inventory/service command, then commit only after that command succeeds. Cross-system consistency requires exclusive ledger ownership and no callbacks/mutations between prepare and commit; the host establishes that synchronous boundary. CafeGame prepares before supplier capacity reservation, pending cancellation release and coffee delivery. It publishes the matching posting before acknowledging a receipt or retiring a supplier record. Inventory and SupplyBoard retain their existing independent contracts.

When history reaches its limit, the oldest entry is compacted into sorted per-stream archived credits, debits and sequence. Stream totals and high-water marks include archived entries, so old source IDs never become chargeable again. entries() exposes the recent suffix; credits(), debits(), balance(), sequence(key), stream(key) and streams() expose totals. The journal is a cash-flow record with bounded detail; its cumulative summaries retain opening cash separately from operating results.

LedgerSnapshot is typed host-owned data: opening, balance, nextEntry, archived stream summaries and recent LedgerEntry records. restore validates the complete candidate: bounds, canonical archived order, source counts and watermarks, contiguous entry IDs, sequence progression, chronological time, integer arithmetic, nonnegative intermediate cash and exact resulting balance. Failed restore preserves the live ledger and quote revision. Successful restore invalidates existing quotes. No bytes or file format are imposed on the core Runtime, whose snapshot schema remains 1. Hosts serialize the typed snapshot in their joint envelope, validate its game-specific stream rules, and publish all state together.

CafeGame envelope 5 includes the ledger, staffed milliseconds, legacy receipt baseline and pending prepaid supply amount. New browser/console shifts start with 50.00 cash, espresso revenue is 5.00, beans cost 1.00 each, and wages accrue at 1.00 per 60 on-shift seconds including idle time. Staff breaks pause accrual; global pause/suspension stop simulation time. Whole unpaid wages remain owed and receive priority over new purchases when funds arrive. Pending cancellation refunds only its prepaid amount. Old formats 1-4 carry historical cash as opening funds, retain a sales baseline and grandfather in-flight free deliveries; wages begin at upgrade. No historical journal is invented. Pricing UI, taxes, loans, rent, multi-currency accounting and insolvency policy are outside this stage.

Contracts cover nonnegative cash, invalid/duplicate/gapped sources, cumulative caps, prepare/commit conservation, stale/foreign/consumed and lifetime-reused candidates, bounded-history compaction, typed restore corruption, supply charge failures, exact refunds, unpaid wages, staff breaks, partitioned accrual, snapshot migration and receipt idempotency. Actual execution evidence is appended after validation; portable source and CI configuration do not establish native or hardware GPU acceptance by themselves.
